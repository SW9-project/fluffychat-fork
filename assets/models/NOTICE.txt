functiongemma-270M-sw9-pilot1.litertlm is a modified version of FunctionGemma 270M
(google/functiongemma-270m-it): the SW9 project fine-tuned it and converted it to
LiteRT-LM. It is a pilot, not for a study.

The fine-tuning data includes utterances from MASSIVE 1.1 by Amazon, which includes
text from SLURP, both licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
We used the plain utterance text of the en-US and da-DK locales, sampled and filtered
it, and labelled it as requests the app's assistant should not act on. This use does
not imply endorsement by Amazon or by the SLURP authors.
